Hot Zones

Top Investment Locations

ROI: 15–18% / yr

Bijalpur (Rau)

The fastest-growing residential corridor in Indore. Excellent connectivity to the ring road, affordable entry prices, and rapid infrastructure development make this the #1 pick for budget investors.

  • ₹25L – ₹60L range
  • Near proposed metro corridor
  • High rental yield: 4–5% per year
ROI: 12–15% / yr

Dhar Road

Excellent connectivity to the Pithampur industrial zone has made Dhar Road a top pick for mid-range investors. Strong workforce housing demand keeps rental yields healthy.

  • ₹22L – ₹45L range
  • Industrial zone proximity
  • High rental demand from workers
ROI: 10–13% / yr

Manik Bagh

A premium residential area with excellent social infrastructure — schools, hospitals, malls. Ideal for mid-range buyers seeking a combination of lifestyle and capital appreciation.

  • ₹55L – ₹95L range
  • Established social infrastructure
  • Premium tenant profile
ROI: 8–12% / yr

Khatiwala Tank

One of Indore's most prestigious addresses. Capital appreciation is steady and the luxury rental market is growing. Perfect for HNI investors seeking prestige and long-term value.

  • ₹1Cr – ₹3Cr+ range
  • Premium luxury segment
  • Corporate rental demand
